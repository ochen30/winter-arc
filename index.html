Discipline methodology

EXPLAINABLE

Score mixes recent execution (35%), day-to-day consistency (25%), growth above a gradually adapting personal baseline (20%), longer-term reliability (15%), and recovery after missed days (5%).

A seven-day calibration precedes your first index. Missing data stays unscored rather than being treated as failure. No extra credit for training on rest days or exceeding safe targets.

Inside your Index

The Discipline Index is a heuristic self-reflection tool, not a clinical or scientific assessment. It avoids fictional precision by showing a calibration state and rounds to whole numbers.

Recent execution (35%) — exponential weighting toward recent eligible days.

Consistency (25%) — mean minus volatility, rewarding stable follow-through.

Improvement (20%) — compares your recent pattern with an exponentially adapting history baseline.

Reliability (15%) — sustained completion across the entire arc so far.

Resilience (5%) — how reliably you return to your routine after an off day.

Sleep and protein use graduated partial credit so narrowly missing a target is not treated like missing it entirely. Unfilled past days are labeled “not logged”; the index only considers days you explicitly saved. The score changes as evidence accumulates; its components are not machine learning.
